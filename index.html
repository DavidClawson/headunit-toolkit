Open Head Unit Toolkit

Opening the firmware configurator… if it doesn't redirect, click here.

An open-source toolkit for customizing cheap Allwinner F133 (“D1s”, Melis RTOS) car stereos — custom boot splash, wallpaper, and menu text. Everything runs in your browser; your firmware is never uploaded.

Source, docs, and the reverse-engineering notes: github.com/DavidClawson/headunit-toolkit

Bring your own firmware image and keep a backup. Not affiliated with any manufacturer. “CarPlay” and “Android Auto” are trademarks of their respective owners.
